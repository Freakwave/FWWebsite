SAFETY & CONTROL FRAMEWORK

HUMAN-IN-THE-LOOP (HITL) GOVERNANCE

Full automation without governance risks compounding hallucinations into expensive hardware failures. In mission-critical systems such as 1000V battery cyclers, HITL checkpoints ensure safety, financial oversight, and deterministic compliance:

CHECKPOINT HITL-01 SCOPE SIGN-OFF

Product Owner approves synthesized requirements, acceptance criteria, and budget impact prior to code generation.

CHECKPOINT HITL-02 EXCEPTION ARBITER

Triggered automatically when an agent exhausts its retry limit or detects hardware safety violations (e.g. HIL loop jitter > 2.0ms).

CHECKPOINT HITL-03 CANARY RELEASE GATE

Final sign-off by Technical Release Arbiter to route live production traffic to 1% canary nodes before full SaaS fleet deployment.
