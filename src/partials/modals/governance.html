SAFETY & CONTROL FRAMEWORK

HUMAN-IN-THE-LOOP (HITL) GOVERNANCE

Full automation without governance risks compounding hallucinations into expensive failures. In mission-critical domains such as battery test systems, human checkpoints keep safety, financial oversight, and compliance deterministic:

CHECKPOINT 01 : INPUT GOAL DEFINITION

A human sets scope boundaries and guardrails, so agents start from an approved intent instead of guessing it.

CHECKPOINT 02 : ON DEMAND HUMAN ADVISOR

Agents raise trade-offs, specification questions, and merge conflicts instead of resolving them silently.

CHECKPOINT 03 : OUTPUT FINAL GATE

A human reviews the consolidated PR dossier and approves the merge, or sends the work back for rework.

The Giemsa Governance Principle

AI agents perform high-velocity exploration, synthesis, and verification. Humans retain ultimate strategic ownership, architectural authority, and safety sign-off.
