FIELD NOTES : HUMAN + AI

What agent design teaches us about working together

The same clarity that helps an AI act reliably helps people collaborate with less friction.

01 / Make the mandate explicit

A role is more than a prompt.

Define the goal, required inputs, decision boundaries, and what to escalate. Clear responsibilities replace guesswork for both agents and teammates.

02 / Match autonomy to risk

Let safe work move; keep consequential choices human.

Analysis and drafts can be reviewed and revised. Actions with real-world consequences need deliberate human approval.

03 / Treat knowledge as infrastructure

Ground answers in maintained sources.

Retrieval can connect an agent to organizational knowledge, but it cannot make stale or contradictory documentation trustworthy. Ownership and review matter.

04 / Turn errors into feedback

Improve the system, not just the answer.

When results miss the mark, check the role, tools, context, and process. A useful review loop helps people and AI learn from the same failure.

Designing for AI is applied organizational development.

Documenting how work gets done can reduce coordination overhead, make knowledge shareable, and give people clearer room to act. AI is valuable when it strengthens that human system.
